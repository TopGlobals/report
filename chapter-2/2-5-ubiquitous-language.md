## 2.5. Ubiquitous Language

<table>
	<thead>
		<tr>
			<th>Ubiquitous Term</th>
			<th>Definition</th>
		</tr>
	</thead>
	<tbody>
		<tr>
			<td><strong>Cold Chain</strong></td>
			<td>The continuous, uninterrupted temperature-controlled clinical storage process that guarantees the viability of the supplies.</td>
		</tr>
		<tr>
			<td><strong>Biological Asset</strong></td>
			<td>Any reagent, vaccine, or patient sample provided with a tracking tag, which is managed and monitored within the facility.</td>
		</tr>
		<tr>
			<td><strong>Designated Compartment</strong></td>
			<td>The specific physical space (shelf, refrigerator, or cold room) assigned to a biological asset, which has a particular thermal range required for its preservation.</td>
		</tr>
		<tr>
			<td><strong>Spatial Misplacement</strong></td>
			<td>A logistical risk event that occurs when a biological asset is detected by the RFID system in a compartment different from the one it was assigned to.</td>
		</tr>
		<tr>
			<td><strong>Thermal Excursion</strong></td>
			<td>A critical event that occurs when a storage compartment deviates outside its safe temperature thresholds.</td>
		</tr>
		<tr>
			<td><strong>Cross-Preventive Alert</strong></td>
			<td>A notification generated by the software when it identifies an incompatibility; for example, a sample deposited in an incorrect location that could trigger its spoilage.</td>
		</tr>
		<tr>
			<td><strong>Telemetry Reading</strong></td>
			<td>The exact data block captured at a specific timestamp, which includes both the current temperature and the location coordinates of the inventory.</td>
		</tr>
		<tr>
			<td><strong>Asset Spoilage</strong></td>
			<td>The irreversible waste, disposal, or economic loss of a biological batch due to a location error or prolonged thermal failure.</td>
		</tr>
		<tr>
			<td><strong>Incident Log</strong></td>
			<td>The immutable record within the system that documents any detected deviation (thermal or location) and the response of the staff in charge.</td>
		</tr>
		<tr>
			<td><strong>Compliance Report</strong></td>
			<td>An official document automatically generated by the application that consolidates the history of thermal and positional traceability in a valid format for quality audits (e.g., ISO 15189).</td>
		</tr>
	</tbody>
</table>
