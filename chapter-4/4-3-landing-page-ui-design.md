## 4.2. Information Architecture

CrioVigil’s information architecture is organized to immediately communicate its value proposition for laboratory monitoring while providing clear, logical pathways for user conversion. The design bridges the public-facing landing page with the internal application dashboard to support seamless navigation for laboratory managers and compliance officers.

### 4.2.1. Organization Systems

The structural hierarchy is divided into the public conversion funnel and the core operational dashboard.

* **Landing Page:** Structured as a continuous, vertically scrolling narrative that guides the user from high-level value to specific features, culminating in transparent pricing and a final conversion point.
  * **Hero Section:** Features the core headline "Smart Monitoring for Safe Laboratories," targeting refrigerators, incubators, and lab equipment. It includes dual Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons ("Try Demo" and "View Features") alongside a high-fidelity preview of the Lab Details dashboard.
  * **Social Proof Bar:** A horizontal banner displaying partner or client logos to establish immediate market trust.
  * **Features Overview:** A three-column grid isolating primary benefits: Real-time Alerts, Compliance Tracking, and Centralized Dashboard.
  * **Integration Showcase:** Pairs the "Seamless Integration with Your Lab" messaging with a dashboard preview highlighting temperature trend charts and environmental parameters.
  * **Compliance Showcase:** Pairs the "Audit-Ready Compliance" messaging with a dashboard preview of the History module, showing alert tallies, resolution status, and chronological event logs.
  * **Pricing Section:** A dual-card layout comparing the Standard tier ($299/mo) against the Enterprise tier (Custom pricing).
  * **Bottom CTA:** A prominent, high-contrast block ("Ready to eliminate temperature risks in your laboratory?") driving users to a final "Request Demo" action.
* **Web Application:** Organized to provide immediate visibility into critical environmental metrics.
  * **Lab Details / Dashboard:** Centralized view showing specific zones (e.g., Lab 01, Lab 02) and active telemetry for Temperature, Humidity, AQI, and Door Status.
  * **Analytics / Trends:** Visual line charts tracking historical parameter stability over selected timeframes.
  * **History & Audit:** Chronological log tracking total alerts, critical breaches, warnings, and resolved incidents to support regulatory compliance.

CrioVigil employs direct, action-oriented labeling across its public interface to minimize cognitive load and drive user engagement.

| **Secondary CTAs** | `View Features` | Low-friction exploration button located in the hero section. |

| **History UI** | `Total Alerts`, `Critical`, `Warning`, `Resolved` | Categorical labels for incident management and audit tracking. |

### 4.2.4. Navigation Systems

Navigation on the CrioVigil landing page is designed to keep the user oriented while offering persistent opportunities to convert.

* **Persistent Top Header:** A clean, fixed navigation bar featuring the CrioVigil logo on the left, centered anchor links (`Features`, `Integration`, `Compliance`, `Pricing`), and a primary `Request Demo` button on the right.
* **Footer Navigation Matrix:** A structured, multi-column directory at the bottom of the page providing secondary access points without cluttering the main narrative:
  * **Product:** `Features`, `Integrations`, `Pricing`, `Changelog`
  * **Company:** `About Us`, `Careers`, `Contact Us`,
  * **Support** `Help Center`, `24/7 Emergency Line`, `Documentation`,`System Status`
  * **Legal:** `FDA 21 CFR Part 11`,`Privacy Policy`, `Terms of Service`, `Security & GDPR`
* **Social Links:** Standard icon-based links located in the bottom-left corner of the footer for brand engagement.
